FAQ

Vinyl Siding Questions

How long does vinyl siding last?

Quality vinyl siding can last 20–40 years with very little maintenance.

Is vinyl siding low maintenance?

Yes — it never needs painting and usually just an occasional rinse to look great.

Can insulated siding lower energy bills?

Insulated vinyl siding adds a layer of protection that can help with energy efficiency.
