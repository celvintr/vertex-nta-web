FAQ

Roofing in Erie, PA — Questions

How long does a new roof last?

A quality asphalt roof lasts 20–30 years and metal 40–70 — we help you pick the right fit for your budget.

How do I know if I need a new roof?

Curling or missing shingles, leaks, granules in the gutters, or a roof over 20 years old are common signs. We inspect for free.

Do you help with insurance claims?

Yes — for storm damage we document everything and work with your adjuster.

Are estimates free?

Yes. We visit, measure and send a written, no-obligation estimate at no cost.
