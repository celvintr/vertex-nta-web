FAQ

Siding in Dover, DE — Questions

Which siding is best?

Vinyl is affordable and low-maintenance; fiber-cement is more durable and fire-resistant. We help you choose for your home and budget.

How long does siding last?

Quality vinyl lasts 20–40 years and fiber-cement 30–50 with minimal upkeep.

Can you match my existing siding?

In most cases yes — we match profile and color as closely as possible for repairs.

Are estimates free?

Yes. We visit, measure and send a written, no-obligation estimate at no cost.
