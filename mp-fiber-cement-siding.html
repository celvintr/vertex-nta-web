FAQ

Fiber Cement Siding Questions

Is fiber cement better than vinyl?

It's more durable and premium, with a real-wood look — but costs more upfront. We help you weigh the options.

How long does fiber cement last?

Typically 30–50 years with minimal maintenance, making it a strong long-term value.

Does it need painting?

It comes pre-finished or paintable and holds paint far longer than wood — with much less upkeep.

Is it worth the cost?

For durability, looks, and resale value, many homeowners find it well worth the investment.
