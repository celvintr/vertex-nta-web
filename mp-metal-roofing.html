FAQ

Metal Roofing Questions

How long does a metal roof last?

A quality metal roof lasts 40–70 years — often two to three times longer than asphalt shingles.

Is metal roofing worth the cost?

Metal costs more upfront but lasts far longer, lowers energy bills, and rarely needs repairs — making it a smart long-term investment.

Is a metal roof noisy in the rain?

No — with proper underlayment and decking, a modern metal roof is no louder than any other roof.

Does metal roofing help with energy bills?

Yes. Metal reflects the sun's heat, which can noticeably reduce cooling costs in summer.
